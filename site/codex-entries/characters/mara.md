Twenty-one years old. Data verifier for The Accord — she checks that the system's outputs match its stated criteria. It is careful, unglamorous work. She is good at it.

She reads people the way she reads data: looking for the gap between what something claims to be and what it actually does.

She found the garden while processing an environmental anomaly flag — the kind of thing she closes dozens of times a week without a second thought. This one, she didn't close so easily.

In June 2047 she is reassigned to Regional Data Standards — worded as workflow optimisation, "not a disciplinary action," but recognised by everyone involved for what it is. The move takes effect in fourteen days and strips her of access to sector-specific case data. She will still be able to walk to Sector 9 as any citizen can; what she loses is the seeing — the flags, the shifts in status, the ability to know what is coming before it arrives.
