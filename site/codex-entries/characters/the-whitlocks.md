A family in Sector 9, among the neighbours Daniel brings to Elias when the corridor's infrastructure review notice goes up. Their tenancy records reach back to 2029: six years running the water point rotation, teaching children who had nowhere else to learn to read.

Elias helps them build their appeal — careful sentences, verifiable facts, no promises. Their case is given a reference number and enters the process.

The answer, when it comes, is a denial: *insufficient demonstrated deviation from model-projected outcomes.* No further appeal available at that determination level. Everything they did counted for nothing the model had not already expected — which, by its own accounting, it never gets wrong.

Elias and Mara deliver the letter to them in person, in the evening. It should not, he says, arrive as a letter alone.
